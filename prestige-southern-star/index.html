Project Overview

Prestige Southern Star is a luxurious residential project spread across 30+ acres in Akshayanagar, Bangalore. The project offers meticulously designed 1, 2, 3, and 4 BHK homes with home offices in select layouts, catering to the evolving needs of modern homebuyers.

With 80% open spaces including a large central park, Prestige Southern Star offers a perfect blend of modern amenities and natural surroundings, creating an ideal living environment for families.
